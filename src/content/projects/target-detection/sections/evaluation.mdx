## Use each split for its intended purpose

Use validation data to compare runs and make training decisions. Reserve the test split for a final check. Review metric curves and rendered validation predictions together; look for missed objects, false alarms, loose boxes, and failures tied to scale or background.

Precision, recall, and mAP answer different questions. Report the dataset version, split, confidence threshold, and relevant metric settings with results.

## Choose the next experiment

- Missed small targets: collect and label more examples at that scale.
- False detections: add representative confusing backgrounds and empty frames.
- Loose or inconsistent boxes: review and correct annotation rules.
- Strong training but weak validation: investigate overfitting, duplicates, and split mismatch.

Change one major factor at a time and record the dataset version, checkpoint, settings, and observed errors.
